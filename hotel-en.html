At the hotel

Information for your stay.

Hotel services organised by topic, with every section open for easy reading.

01

Arrival and departure

Check-in from 15:00. Check-out by 12:00. We can store your luggage at reception so you can enjoy your time before check-in or after departure.

02

Reception and telephone

Reception is available 24 hours a day. From your room telephone, dial 9 for reception or the room number to call another room. For external calls, dial 0 followed by the number; charges are added to your room bill. A wake-up call can be requested.

03

Breakfast

07:30–10:00 · Salão Rossio, floor 0. Please tell the team about any special dietary requirements before your meal.

04

Restaurant

12:30–14:30 | 19:30–21:30 · Salão Épico, floor 0. Ask the restaurant team about the à la carte menu, daily menu and dietary requirements. Always tell the team about allergies or intolerances before ordering.

05

Grevin Bar

16:00–23:00 · Floor 0. Drinks and light meals for an afternoon or evening break.

06

Swimming pools

10:00–20:00. The indoor pool is open all year; the outdoor pool opens seasonally. Pool towels can be requested at reception. Follow the posted instructions and always supervise children.

07

Room and housekeeping

Cleaning starts at 09:00. Bed linen and towels are changed twice a week and between guests. Use the “Do not disturb” sign when needed. If it remains on the door until 16:30, your room will not be serviced that day; fresh towels are available from reception. You may also request hangers and a second key.

08

Comfort and services

Free Wi-Fi in rooms and shared areas; ask reception for access details. Adjust the air conditioning in your room and keep windows closed while it is running. Shampoo, shower gel and soap are provided. Dental, sewing and shaving kits, adapters and transformers are available on request. For laundry and ironing, use the bag and form in your room.

09

Families

Cots and extra beds are subject to availability. High chairs are available on request. For help preparing or warming children’s meals, hot water or sterilising utensils, contact reception or the restaurant.

10

Parking and accessibility

Private outdoor parking with two spaces reserved for vehicles used by people with reduced mobility. For accessibility assistance, luggage, taxis and local directions, contact reception.

11

Safety and assistance

In the event of fire, follow the emergency exit signs and do not use the lift. A first-aid kit is available at reception, where staff can also direct you to pharmacies and medical services. A central safe at reception is available for identified and sealed valuables.

12

Lost property

Lost property is kept for one year. Items can be sent on request at the guest’s expense. Contact reception with a description of the item and the dates of your stay.

13

Consideration for others

Smoking is not permitted in rooms or indoor areas. Wear appropriate clothing in shared spaces and respect other guests’ rest. Animals are not permitted, except guide dogs. To reuse your towels, leave them on the rails.

Services on request are subject to availability. Reception can assist you throughout your stay.
